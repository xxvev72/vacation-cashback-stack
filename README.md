## Where the actual vacation package deals are right now

Package deals earn their discount from bundling: when you buy flights and a hotel together — sometimes with a rental car added — suppliers pass along a combined rate that's lower than booking each piece separately. Expedia states that One Key members can get up to 30% off when they combine a hotel reservation with a flight, which makes the bundle itself the first discount layer.

The second layer is timing. Expedia runs a predictable seasonal sale cycle, and if you can match your booking window to one of these, the same package gets cheaper:

- **Annual Vacation Sale** (February–March): up to 40% off select hotels for One Key members
- **Family Getaway Sale**: 25%+ off accommodations for One Key members, with booking deadlines and travel windows tied to the end of September
- **End of Summer Sale** (August): 25%+ off select hotels and resorts
- **Black Friday Sale** (November): up to 50% off select hotels and vacation rentals
- **Cyber Monday Sale**: continues the Black Friday discounts across hotels, flights, packages, and car rentals
- **Travel Tuesday Sale** (right after Cyber Monday): up to 50% off select hotels, flights, packages, and car rentals

Beyond Expedia, dedicated packagers like Delta Vacations, Funjet, and Apple Vacations run their own bundles — all-inclusive Mexico and Caribbean deals are their bread and butter. Those can be worth comparing if you already know your destination. The difference is that Expedia's rates are easy to verify through a cash back portal, while some tour operators don't participate in cash back programs at all. Check before you commit either way.

## Who shouldn't bother with this

Honest answer time. The cash back layer isn't worth your attention if any of these describe you:

- **You only buy flights.** A flat $2.50 regardless of ticket price isn't a reason to change your booking habits.
- **You need the money back quickly.** Between pending periods and travel confirmation, you're looking at weeks to months.
- **You book primarily with points.** Cash back programs pay on cash transactions, and bookings paid with rewards points are commonly excluded — check the specific offer terms before relying on it.

For everyone else booking actual packages — flight + hotel, all-inclusives, family trips to Orlando — the math is simple. A 5% rate on a $1,500 bundle is $75. Same room, same flights, same price on Expedia.
